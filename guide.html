How to stop spam calls on an iPhone in Australia

A plain guide. Most of it is free and built into your iPhone. No app blocks every scam call, and this guide won't pretend otherwise.

1. What your telco already does

Australian telcos must block many known scam calls before they reach you, under rules from the Australian Communications and Media Authority (ACMA, the phone regulator). What still rings is what got past them.

2. Free: block one number

  1. Open Phone → Recents.
  2. Tap the (i) next to the number.
  3. Scroll down and tap Block this Caller.

Works well for one number. Spam callers often switch to another number next time.

3. Free: Silence Unknown Callers

Settings → Apps → Phone → Silence Unknown Callers. Anyone not in your contacts goes straight to voicemail.

The catch: the doctor's surgery, the school, a courier or a tradie also go to voicemail.

4. Block a whole range of numbers

When the calls come from numbers that differ only in the last few digits (for example 02 9123 4501, 02 9123 4577, 02 9123 4590), you can block the whole batch instead of one at a time. That's what Spamurai does:

  1. Copy one of the numbers from Recents and paste it into Spamurai.
  2. Choose how many of the last digits can change. 2 digits = 100 numbers, 3 digits = 1,000.
  3. Switch Spamurai on once in Settings → Apps → Phone → Call Blocking & Identification.

Blocked calls never ring. Everyone else still gets through. Your list stays on your phone: no account, nothing sent anywhere.

Keep ranges small. A wide range can also block a real business in it, so add anyone you need to Never block first. The app warns you when a range is large.

5. Report scams

Report scam calls to Scamwatch (scamwatch.gov.au). It helps others get warned.

Support

Spamurai is a private call blocker for iPhone. This page covers setup and the questions people ask most. Everything stays on your phone, so there is no account to manage.

Set it up (about a minute)

  1. Open Spamurai.
  2. Go to Settings > Apps > Phone > Call Blocking & Identification.
  3. Switch Spamurai on.

iOS (the iPhone's operating system) keeps call blockers off until you allow them. Until you do, the app shows a red banner with a Turn it on button that opens that screen.

Block a number

  1. A spam call comes in. In Phone > Recents, tap the (i) next to the call.
  2. Hold the number and choose Copy.
  3. Open Spamurai and tap Paste, or tap + then Add number and paste it.
  4. Save. Blocking is live within a few seconds.

For a faster way, use the Block a Number action in the Shortcuts app. You can put it on your Home Screen or Action Button.

Block a whole batch

Spam callers often cycle through neighbouring numbers. A "batch" is a run of those neighbours.

  1. Add the number as above.
  2. Under Also catch, choose how many of its last digits to cover (1 to 6).
  3. Check the count shown before you save. For example, the last 2 digits covers 100 numbers.

You can also swipe left on a blocked number in the list and tap Widen. Spamurai may also suggest a batch when several of your blocked numbers look like one run.

A batch can include numbers that belong to real people. If a contact falls inside a blocked batch, add them to the Never block list.

Undo or change a block

  • Right after you block a number, tap Undo on the confirmation.
  • Later, swipe left on the number in the list and tap Delete.
  • Not sure about a number? Swipe and choose Label instead. It will ring, but show "Likely spam" or your own words.
  • Want a built-in list off? Switch it off under Built-in protection. The two older reported-scam lists start switched off.

Why does a blocked call still show in Recents?

That is how iOS works. A blocked call does not ring, but iOS still logs it in Phone > Recents, marked Blocked. Nothing is hidden from you. Spamurai does not control the call log and cannot remove those entries.

Back up and restore

  1. In the app, tap Back up list. The share sheet opens with your list as text.
  2. Send it somewhere you can find it later, such as Notes or Files.
  3. On a new phone, copy that text, open Spamurai and tap Paste a list. Paste it in. It accepts a Spamurai backup as well as plain numbers.

This is shared or pasted text. It is not an automatic sync between devices.

Free and full version

The free version blocks up to 5 numbers and includes the built-in list of fake numbers. The full version is a one-off A$4.99 purchase inside the app. It adds as many numbers as you like, number ranges, the reported-scam lists, the lookalike guard and pasting in a list.

The free limit only stops you adding more. Nothing already on your list is removed, and it keeps blocking.

New phone, or reinstalled? Open the unlock screen and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with. You are not charged again.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What Spamurai cannot do

  • It does not stop every scam call. It blocks the numbers you give it, plus a built-in list of fake numbers.
  • It cannot read your call log. You copy numbers from Recents.
  • It cannot block "No Caller ID" calls. Use Silence Unknown Callers in iOS.
  • The lookalike guard labels calls ("Possible spoof"). It does not block them.

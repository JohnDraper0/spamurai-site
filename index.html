Questions

Does it stop every spam call?

No. It blocks the numbers you give it, plus a built-in list of fake numbers that no real caller uses. Add numbers as they call.

Why do I add numbers myself?

Apple does not let call blocking apps read your call log. You copy a number from Recents and paste it in. The Shortcuts action makes it quick.

Can it block "No Caller ID" calls?

No. Apple does not allow it. Use the Silence Unknown Callers setting in iOS for those.

What about the scam lists inside the app?

The list of fake numbers is on by default. Two older lists of reported scam numbers, from 2021 and 2022, start switched off. Numbers get reassigned, and some may belong to real people now.

Does it work outside Australia?

Number formats are Australian for now. Numbers from other countries work if you start them with + or 0011.

Can I move my list to a new phone?

Yes. Back up shares your list as text. On the new phone, paste it back in.

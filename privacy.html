Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Spamurai is a call blocker for iPhone. It is made by an independent developer in Australia ("the developer"). This policy says what the app does with your information. Short answer: nothing. The developer does not collect it.

What we collect

Nothing. Spamurai has no account, no sign-up, no server, no analytics and no ads. The developer never receives your numbers, your calls or any other data from the app.

What stays on your phone

  • Your block list. The numbers you block, label or allow are saved on your iPhone. Apple's call blocking feature (called a Call Directory extension) reads that list on the phone so iOS can stop the call before it rings.
  • Your own number, if you use the lookalike guard. You can type your mobile number so Spamurai can flag spoofed numbers that start like yours. It is saved on your iPhone only.
  • Settings. For example, which built-in lists are switched on.

Spamurai does not see who calls you. iOS compares each incoming call with your list on the phone. The app is not told the result.

Spamurai does not ask for, or read, your contacts or your call history. You copy numbers from Recents and paste them in yourself.

Built-in lists

The built-in lists are part of the app. They are not downloaded. Spamurai does not connect to the internet to update them. A new list only arrives with an app update.

Backups and sharing

"Back up list" uses the standard iPhone share sheet. It turns your list into text. Where that text goes is your choice, for example Notes, Files or Messages. Once you send it somewhere, that service's own privacy policy applies. The developer does not see it.

Shortcuts and Siri

The "Block a Number" action in Shortcuts adds a number to your list on the phone. Apple handles Siri and Shortcuts under its own privacy terms.

Buying the full version

The one-off unlock is bought through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple handles the payment under its own privacy terms. The app asks Apple only whether the unlock has been bought on your Apple Account. The developer never sees your name, your payment details or your list. This is the only time the app uses the internet.

Information from Apple

Apple may give the developer anonymous sales and crash summaries through App Store Connect, if you have agreed to share them with app developers in your iPhone settings. These do not identify you and do not contain your list.

Children

Spamurai collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and your list goes with it. You can also delete single numbers inside the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new date. Because the app collects nothing, a change would be announced in the app's release notes too.
